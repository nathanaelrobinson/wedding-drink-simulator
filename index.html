Consumption Profiles (drinks/hr, Gamma)

Calibrated from CDC/NIAAA population data + catering industry benchmarks. Industry rule of thumb: 2 drinks in hour 1, then 1/hr after = ~6 over 5 hrs for an average drinker. Top 10% of US drinkers consume ~74% of all alcohol (Cook, Duke Univ.). Wedding demo (25–40) skews heavier than general pop.
Sources: CDC NCHS Data Brief #374, NIAAA, Philip J. Cook "Paying the Tab", Pew 2024

How to read: "Moderate" at 1.2/hr = ~6 drinks over 5 hours, which matches the catering industry standard. A "Whale" at 2.4/hr = ~12 drinks (your friend who has 11 beers fits here). The std dev controls spread — lower = more predictable, higher = more variance.
Wedding demo prior (CDC-adjusted): ~20% non-drinker, ~35% light, ~30% moderate, ~12% heavy, ~3% whale. General US adult pop is 34% non-drinker, but wedding guests (25–40, celebratory setting) skew higher consumption.
